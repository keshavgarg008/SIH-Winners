The Legacy Infrastructure Problem

The traditional hospitality sector is plagued by outdated, physical identification methods that frustrate guests and endanger data security. We solve this.

01

Checkin Congestion

Guests endure exhausting queues at the reception desk to complete tedious physical paperwork. Manual ID verification bottlenecks operations, ruining the initial hospitality experience.

02

Identity Forgery

Fraudsters easily exploit legacy systems using stolen or sophisticated fake ID cards to book rooms. Hotels lack the real-time biometric tools to authenticate physical documents accurately.

03

Data Leak Vulnerability

Physical ID photocopies and unsecured digital scans pile up in hotel back-offices, leaving highly sensitive personal data exposed to insider theft, loss, and massive cybersecurity breaches.

Zero-Knowledge Privacy

Your data is yours. We engineered our architecture so that hotels never hold your identity.

Encrypted Biometric Flags

Hotels never receive your actual Government ID or raw biometric data.

When you scan your face at the DigiVishram Kiosk, our decentralized vault only transmits a secure, encrypted verification "flag" (True/False). Your identity is verified instantly without ever being exposed, stored, or copied by the hotel terminal.

The Yugantar Vision

We started this project because we believe physical documents belong in the past. DigiVishram is more than just a hotel check-in app; it is the foundation for a completely frictionless, decentralized identity ecosystem.

Our long-term mission is to eliminate physical barriers across all sectors of public infrastructure, ensuring that your face is the only key you ever need to travel, stay, and live securely.
